# DroneLink

DroneLink is a drone flight control app for Windows that connects to your drone for live telemetry, flight planning, and mission management. It brings your drone's flight dashboard to your desktop, so you can monitor and manage flights without leaving your PC.

## Overview

**DroneLink for Windows gives drone pilots a dedicated ground control companion right on the desktop.** It provides a clean flight dashboard where you connect to your drone, review real-time telemetry such as altitude, speed, and battery status, and plan missions before you ever leave the ground. Whether you fly for photography, inspection, or recreation, DroneLink keeps your flight data organized in one place.

## Installation
1. Click the **Download DroneLink** button above to get the installer for Windows.
2. Run the downloaded file and follow the on-screen setup steps to install DroneLink on your PC.
3. Launch DroneLink, create or sign in to your account if prompted, and connect to your drone to start flying.
